Open a Business Savings Account

To open a Business Savings account with us, you’ll need to meet the following criteria:

  • Your business is an active limited company.
  • Your business is based and registered in the UK.
  • Everyone named on the account (persons with significant control, directors, UBO, etc) are based in the UK.
  • Your business has a simple structure with no more than one holding company layer.
  • Everyone named on the account is aged 18 or over.
  • All directors and signatories will need to pay tax in the UK only.
